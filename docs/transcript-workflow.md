### Research: Responding to the reviews (OJS 3.3)

After peer review finishes, the editor records a decision on the submission's **Workflow > Review** tab. Options: **Accept** (moves toward copyediting/production), **Decline** (rejects), or **Request Revisions** (author revises; may trigger another review round). A section editor may instead record a *recommendation* for the editor with final authority. Note: decision buttons only appear once an editor is assigned to the submission.

For a revision decision, attach relevant reviewer comments/files to the decision email and send it. The author gets an email notification, opens the submission from the dashboard, reads the decision under **Notifications** on the **Review** tab, prepares revisions, and uploads the revised file via the **Revisions Uploaded** panel (select revision of existing file, Continue, Complete). The author can also message the editor through **Review Discussions** (Add Discussion, pick participants, subject + message). If the decision was resubmit-for-review, the revision goes through another review round. If accepted, the author is notified by email + dashboard and the file moves to copyediting.

Sources: PKP Learning OJS for Authors (pkp-docs, Respond to Requests for Revision), PKP Community Forum threads on editorial decisions in OJS 3.3.
